🧬 BPC-157: Body Protection Compound

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a protective protein naturally found in gastric tissue. In animal and laboratory models it activates VEGFR2, nitric oxide synthase, and focal adhesion kinase complexes — signaling networks central to tissue repair, new blood vessel formation, and cellular survival. Its broad signaling profile across multiple tissue and cellular models makes it one of the most widely studied recovery peptides.

🧬 TB-500: Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment

TB-500 corresponds to the active fragment of thymosin beta-4, an endogenous protein. It interacts with actin-binding pathways that influence cell movement, cytoskeletal reorganization, and vascular signaling. While BPC-157 acts locally, TB-500 engages broader systemic signaling and cellular migration — making the two peptides complementary for comprehensive tissue recovery.

🤝 Why the Blend is Studied: Local + Systemic Recovery

BPC-157 may support local tissue signaling and repair pathways, while TB-500 may engage broader systemic signaling and cellular migration networks. Combining them allows investigation of both local and systemic mechanisms in tissue recovery models — particularly relevant for complex, multi-tissue injuries like multiple shoulder surgeries. Preclinical studies report influence on inflammation modulation, cellular migration, angiogenesis, and connective tissue signaling.

🧬 What is AOD-9604?

AOD-9604 is a synthetic peptide fragment derived from the C-terminal region of human growth hormone (hGH), specifically amino acids 176–191 with an additional tyrosine for stability. It was developed to isolate the fat-burning properties of hGH without its growth-promoting or insulin-disrupting effects.

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

Research suggests AOD-9604 engages adipose tissue signaling by:

  • Stimulating lipolysis — activating fat breakdown pathways
  • Inhibiting lipogenesis — blocking fat storage formation
  • Enhanced activation of beta-3 adrenergic receptors in fat cells

Critically, it does not appear to affect blood sugar or IGF-1 levels the way full HGH does.

🔬 Research Findings & Clinical Notes

In animal studies, AOD-9604 has been reported to increase lipolytic sensitivity and reduce fat accumulation in genetically obese rodent models. Early human trials examined safety and metabolic outcomes. AOD-9604 is not FDA-approved for human use and is sold strictly as a research compound.

🧬 What is Tesamorelin?

Tesamorelin is a synthetic analogue of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) consisting of 44 amino acids with a trans-3-hexenoic acid modification. It is FDA-approved as Egrifta for reducing excess abdominal fat in HIV patients with lipodystrophy — the most clinically studied peptide on this list.

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

Tesamorelin binds to GHRH receptors on somatotroph cells in the anterior pituitary, promoting endogenous GH synthesis and release, which then drives:

  • Increased IGF-1 production
  • Stimulation of hormone-sensitive lipase
  • Reduced visceral adipose tissue
  • Potential reduction of liver fat distribution

폿 Why Tesamorelin for Visceral Fat & Body Recomposition

Unlike AOD-9604 which acts directly on fat cells, Tesamorelin works upstream — signaling the pituitary to release GH naturally. Research has specifically demonstrated effects on visceral adipose tissue and liver fat distribution.

🧬 CJC-1295 (without DAC)

CJC-1295 (without DAC), also known as Modified GRF (1-29), is a GHRH analogue that binds GHRH receptors on pituitary somatotroph cells, activating cAMP/PKA signaling pathways. Without the Drug Affinity Complex, it has a shorter half-life (~30–60 min), producing a pulsatile GH release pattern that more closely mimics natural physiology.

🧬 Ipamorelin

Ipamorelin activates the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) — a completely different pathway from GHRH — triggering calcium mobilization and GH release from pituitary cells. Research indicates it does not significantly elevate cortisol, prolactin, or ACTH at research doses.

💡 Why the Combination is Powerful for Muscle & Bone Preservation

CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin work on separate but complementary GH-stimulating pathways. When used together, they produce a stronger, synergistic GH pulse. This elevated GH drives IGF-1 production, which is the primary downstream signal for: muscle protein synthesis, satellite cell activation, bone remodeling, cellular repair, and fat mobilization.

🧬 What is IGF-LR3?

IGF-LR3 (IGF-1 Long Arg3) is a synthetic analogue of human insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1). It differs from native IGF-1 by having an arginine substituted at position 3 and an extra 13 amino acids at the N-terminus. These changes reduce its affinity for IGF binding proteins and extend its activity.

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

IGF-LR3 interacts with IGF-1 receptors on muscle cell surfaces, triggering the PI3K/Akt signaling pathway. This pathway is involved in:

  • Protein synthesis
  • Amino acid uptake
  • Satellite cell activation
  • Glucose uptake

💪 Why IGF-LR3 is the Most Targeted Muscle Preservation Peptide

While CJC-1295/Ipamorelin and Tesamorelin work by stimulating your pituitary to produce GH, IGF-LR3 skips the middleman and acts directly at the muscle cell level. This is particularly valuable during a caloric deficit. Its longer half-life vs. native IGF-1 means more sustained receptor interaction per dose.

⚠️ Cycling Note

Due to potential receptor desensitization with prolonged use, IGF-LR3 is typically used in cycles (commonly 4 weeks on, 4 weeks off) in research protocols.

🧬 What is MOTS-C?

MOTS-C is a mitochondrial-derived peptide (MDP) encoded directly in mitochondrial DNA. Discovered in 2015, it consists of 16 amino acids and is one of the first peptides identified that transmits signals from mitochondria to the cell nucleus under metabolic stress conditions.

⚙️ Mechanism of Action

MOTS-C engages the folate-AICAR-AMPK pathway — the same core pathway activated by exercise and caloric restriction. Key effects studied include:

  • Glucose uptake in skeletal muscle
  • Improved insulin sensitivity
  • Lipid metabolism regulation
  • Mitochondrial energy use in muscle

⚡ Why MOTS-C for Fat Loss Without Muscle Loss

MOTS-C is uniquely positioned as an "exercise mimetic" at the cellular level — it activates AMPK, which simultaneously tells your cells to burn fat as fuel AND protect lean tissue. MOTS-C expression in the body naturally increases with exercise.

🧬 What is 5-Amino-1MQ?

5-Amino-1MQ is a synthetic small-molecule compound that inhibits nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) — an enzyme that consumes nicotinamide during methylation processes. By blocking NNMT, more nicotinamide remains available for NAD+ synthesis.

⚙️ Why This Matters for Fat Loss

Higher NAD+ levels from NNMT inhibition translate to:

  • Improved mitochondrial energy production
  • Enhanced fat cell metabolism
  • Better metabolic efficiency
  • No stimulant pathways — works at the enzymatic level

🔩 Why 5-Amino-1MQ is Unique in This Stack

Unlike every other compound in this guide which works through peptide receptor signaling, 5-Amino-1MQ operates at a completely different level — enzyme inhibition and NAD+ pathway modulation. It's also one of the few compounds here available in capsule form — no injection required.
